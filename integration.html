Why Calculus Works

Notice how at $n=2$, the staircase rectangles leave huge empty gaps or stick out above the curve. But when $n=40$, the width of each slice $\Delta x = \frac{b-a}{n}$ becomes microscopic, leaving practically zero gap! That is the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
