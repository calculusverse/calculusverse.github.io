MODULE 01 · FOUNDATIONS

Precalculus & Functions

Beginner · 10 min exploration · Algebra prerequisite

Understand how equations bend, stretch, and shift across Cartesian coordinates. Discover the secant line between two points and see what happens when the gap $\Delta x$ shrinks toward zero.

Core Geometric Idea
$m_{\text{secant}} = \frac{f(x + \Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x} \longrightarrow f'(x)$
Explore Module 1
MODULE 02 · RATES OF CHANGE

Differential Calculus & Tangents

Core Foundation · 15 min exploration · Velocity Physics

Watch functions draw in an animated 3-second sweep! Slide along curves to calculate exact instantaneous tangent slopes and watch a synchronized car drive across a road based on $s(t)$ and $v(t)$.

Instantaneous Velocity
$v(t) = \frac{ds}{dt} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{s(t+\Delta t) - s(t)}{\Delta t}$
Explore Module 2
MODULE 03 · ACCUMULATION

Integral Calculus & Riemann Sums

Area Under Curves · 12 min exploration · $n=1$ to $50$ Slices

Estimate irregular curved shapes using rectangular bars. Compare Left, Right, Midpoint, and Trapezoidal rules in real-time, observing how numerical sums converge directly into the definite integral.

Definite Integral Limit
$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n f(x_i^*)\,\Delta x$
Explore Module 3
MODULE 04 · 3D SPACE

3D Multivariable & Gradients

Advanced Visual · 15 min exploration · Three.js WebGL

Ascend beyond flat 2D planes into 3D topography. Rotate saddles and ripple waves with your mouse, manipulate coordinate probes, balance a dynamic tangent plane, and follow the steepest gradient vector.

3D Gradient Vector
$\nabla f(x, y) = \left\langle \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \right\rangle$

APP 01 · KINEMATICS 🚗

Instantaneous Velocity (Speedometer)

Why average speed isn't enough. Find exact speedometer readings from position $s(t) = 2t^2 + 4t$.

$$v(t) = s'(t) = 4t + 4$$

APP 02 · OPTIMIZATION 📦

Maximizing Box Volume (Packaging)

Cut square corners from a $20 \times 12\text{ cm}$ sheet to maximize packaging storage volume.

$$V'(x) = 0 \implies x \approx 2.43\text{ cm}$$
Open Box Lab
APP 03 · ACCUMULATION 🚅

Total Distance from Speed (Express Train)

Accumulating the area under changing velocity $v(t) = -15t^2 + 60t + 80$ over $4$ hours.

$$\text{Dist} = \int_0^4 v(t)\,dt = 480\text{ km}$$
Open Train Lab
APP 04 · AEROSPACE 🚀

Work Done by Variable Force (Rocket)

Lifting a fuel-burning rocket $m(x) = 2,000 - 0.1x$ from $0$ to $10,000\text{ m}$ in Earth gravity.

$$W = \int_0^{10000} 9.8(2000 - 0.1x)\,dx = 147\text{ MJ}$$
Open Rocket Lab
APP 05 · RELATED RATES 🪐

Escape Velocity & Related Rates

Finding tool acceleration $\frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{dv}{dr}\frac{dr}{dt}$ at $1,500\text{ km}$ above a lunar outpost.

$$a = \frac{GM}{r^2} \approx 1.78\text{ m/s}^2$$
Open Orbital Lab
APP 06 · ECONOMICS 📱

Marginal Profit Analysis (Smartphones)

Finding where Marginal Revenue equals Marginal Cost ($MR = MC$) for peak $\$1.24\text{M}$ weekly profit.

$$MR = MC \implies x = 250\text{k units}$$
Open Profit Lab
APP 07 · MARKET SURPLUS 🚕

Consumer Surplus (Rideshare Pricing)

Integrating savings under demand curve $p(x) = 40 - 0.05x$ at $\$15$ equilibrium to find $\$6.25\text{M}$ surplus.

$$CS = \int_0^{500} (25 - 0.05x)\,dx = \$6,250,000$$
Open Surplus Lab

How to Get the Most Out of CalculusVerse

1. Play Before You Read

Drag the sliders first! Watch how the curves flex and how the car accelerates. Building physical intuition first makes formulas instantly obvious.

2. Notice the "Speedometer" Analogy

In Module 2, the derivative $f'(x)$ is nothing more than your speedometer reading at a frozen millisecond in time.

3. Watch Slices Vanish

In Module 3, slide $n$ from 3 to 50. Notice how the staircase of rectangular blocks smooths out to match the real curve with near 0% error.
