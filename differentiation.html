The "Speedometer" Secret

If a highway police officer clocks your speed, they don't look at how far you drove across 3 hours. They check your instantaneous rate of change right at that moment!

  • Positive Slope ($f' > 0$): Car drives forward.
  • Negative Slope ($f' < 0$): Car reverses backwards.
  • Zero Slope ($f' = 0$): Car comes to a momentary full stop!
