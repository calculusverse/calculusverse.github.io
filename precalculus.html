10th Grade Takeaway

In algebra, you learned $\text{Slope} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ for straight lines. Calculus simply lets $x_2$ get infinitely close to $x_1$, turning that average secant slope into the instantaneous slope of a single point!
