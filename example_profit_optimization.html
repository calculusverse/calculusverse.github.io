Marginal Analysis: Maximizing Smartphone Profit

Why producing more doesn't always make more money. Discover the golden business rule of derivatives: maximum profit occurs when Marginal Revenue equals Marginal Cost ($MR = MC$).

The Real-Life Problem

A tech hardware company manufactures smartphones. If they produce and sell $x$ thousand smartphones per week, their weekly revenue $R(x)$ and total manufacturing cost $C(x)$ (both measured in thousands of dollars) are modeled by:

Total Revenue $R(x)$
$$R(x) = 12x - 0.02x^2$$
Total Cost $C(x)$
$$C(x) = 2x + 10$$

Target Question:
How many smartphones should the factory produce each week to achieve the maximum possible profit, and what will that peak profit be?

Production Strategies:
💡 Why Parallel Tangents Guarantee Max Profit

Profit is the vertical gap between the Revenue curve and Cost line: $P(x) = R(x) - C(x)$. The gap is widest where their slopes are identical: $R'(x) = C'(x)$. If $MR > MC$, making one more phone adds profit. If $MR < MC$, making one more phone loses money!

Step-by-Step Mathematical Solution

How calculus provides bulletproof business optimization using derivatives:

Step 1: Construct the Total Profit Function $P(x)$

Profit equals total revenue minus total manufacturing cost:

$$P(x) = R(x) - C(x)$$ $$P(x) = (12x - 0.02x^2) - (2x + 10)$$ $$P(x) = -0.02x^2 + 10x - 10$$

Notice that $P(x)$ is a quadratic downward-opening parabola with leading coefficient $-0.02 < 0$. This confirms the vertex must be an absolute maximum!

Step 2: Take the First Derivative (Marginal Profit $P'(x)$)

Differentiating term-by-term using the power rule:

$$P'(x) = \frac{d}{dx}[-0.02x^2 + 10x - 10] = -0.04x + 10$$

To locate critical points, set the derivative equal to zero:

$$-0.04x + 10 = 0 \implies 0.04x = 10$$ $$x = \frac{10}{0.04} = 250\text{ thousand smartphones (250,000 units)}$$

Notice that this is identical to finding where Marginal Revenue equals Marginal Cost: $$R'(x) = 12 - 0.04x, \quad C'(x) = 2 \implies 12 - 0.04x = 2 \implies x = 250$$

Step 3: Verify the Maximum & Calculate Total Dollar Profit

Check the Second Derivative Test:

$$P''(x) = \frac{d}{dx}[-0.04x + 10] = -0.04 < 0 \quad (\text{Strictly Concave Downward everywhere})$$

Now plug $x = 250$ into $P(x)$, $R(x)$, and $C(x)$:

$$P(250) = -0.02(250)^2 + 10(250) - 10$$ $$P(250) = -0.02(62,500) + 2,500 - 10 = -1,250 + 2,500 - 10$$ $$P(250) = 1,240\text{ thousand dollars} = \$1,240,000$$

Final Answer: The smartphone factory should produce 250,000 phones per week ($x = 250$). At this level, weekly revenue is $\$1,750,000$, production costs are $\$510,000$, resulting in the peak net profit of $\$1,240,000$ per week.
